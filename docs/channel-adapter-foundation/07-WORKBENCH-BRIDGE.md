# Workbench Bridge

The bridge creates a trusted channel context only after account, signature, deduplication, identity, and traffic checks succeed. It forwards a bounded message, locale, server-owned capabilities, correlation ID, and deterministic idempotency key. Ephemeral reply tokens and raw channel objects are not forwarded.

The Workbench remains the sole plan, tool, confirmation, permission, module, and Domain invocation authority. The Channel Adapter cannot bypass confirmation or call Domain repositories directly.
