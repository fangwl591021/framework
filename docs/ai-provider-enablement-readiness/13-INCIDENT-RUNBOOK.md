# Provider Incident Runbook

Runbooks cover credential compromise, outage, rate limiting, excessive cost, unsafe output, region or retention violation, policy change, model retirement, and latency degradation.

They contain safe signals, bounded actions, kill-switch scope, audience, Support Code/Incident references, rollback reference, recovery criteria, review steps, and owner. They do not store Prompt, Response, Secret, SQL, Stack, or execute Production operations. Telegram remains disabled.
