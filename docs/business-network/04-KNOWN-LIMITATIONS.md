# Business Network Engine Known Limitations

- Contract remains Proposed／Pending Tony Approval.
- No public API, UI, provider Adapter, LINE integration or Production scheduler.
- No money movement, payout, settlement, tax, invoice or accounting ledger.
- Only one-level First Valid Touch attribution with a fixed 30-day window.
- No multi-level commission, hierarchy roll-up, MLM, rank, bonus or split attribution.
- A refund and Commission reversal are explicit separate commands; no background automation is assumed.
- Percentage rate uses basis points; other precision models require a future Contract revision.
- Currency conversion is unsupported.
- No Remote D1／Production verification, load evidence or retention approval.
- Domain Permission registration mechanism requires Architecture／Authorization review before Contract approval.

These limitations are deliberate MVP boundaries, not implied future commitments.
